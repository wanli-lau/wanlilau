# Wanlilau

Build a simple, professional website for Wanli Consultancy LLC, a lifestyle coach in Seattle, WA. Make it calm, clean, and easy to read on phones. 

Include: a short description of my services, my hours (4pm to 10 pm PT), and a contact section with phone 206-393-8345, email wanli26@gmail.com, and address 752 Summerhill Ridge Dr NW, Issaquah WA. Add a button near the top labeled "Contact us to book" that scrolls down to the contact section. Put a footer on every page with links to "Terms and Conditions" (/terms) and "Privacy Policy" (/privacy). Do not add logins, payments, forms, testimonials, prices, or photos of people.
